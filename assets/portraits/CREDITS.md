## Not included here

No illustration from nobelprize.org is reproduced in this repository. The Nobel Committee's
figures (© The Nobel Committee for Physiology or Medicine, Ill. Mattias Karlén) are free for
non-commercial use with attribution, but that is not compatible with an MIT-licensed repo, so
every diagram and animation on this page was drawn from scratch in code instead. The page links
out to the official figures rather than copying them.
